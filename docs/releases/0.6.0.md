it is self-review, not a claim of a separate human or model review. No independent
reviewer was dispatched. Findings corrected before release:

- Shared guidance, procedure bodies, workflow acceptance and requirement pages disagreed
  about fresh commit requests, quarantine and the meaning of In Review. They now agree.
- The generated agent template prohibited even explicitly authorized assignment commits.
  It now respects bounded permission without granting delegation or external authority.

  evidence for the same scope and records executor identity and independence.

  changed to satisfy those assumptions.
